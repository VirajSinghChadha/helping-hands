Why it matters

Poverty and child labour form a cycle.

When a family is poor, children often work to help. Working children miss school, so as adults they can only find low-paid jobs, and their own children face the same choice.

Raise family income

Cash transfers, livestock and job training mean parents can earn enough without their children's wages.

Rescue and protect children

Groups find children in dangerous work, bring them to safety and check that factories stay child-labour free.

Feed children at school

A free school meal gives families a strong reason to send children to class instead of work.

Keep children learning

Books, teachers and support for girls help children finish school and find better jobs.

Before you give

Give safely.

Scams copy real charities. These habits make sure your money goes where you mean it to.

  • Donate only on the charity's own website. Every button here goes straight there.
  • Check that the address bar shows the charity's real web address and a lock icon.
  • If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before giving money online.
  • Look a charity up on Charity Navigator or GiveWell before you give.
  • Never pay someone who contacts you first and pressures you to give fast.
