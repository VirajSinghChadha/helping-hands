# Helping Hands — Service as Action record

Use this record to document the website work in a service portfolio. Add dates, hours and evidence from your own work; none are assumed here.

## Project aim

Make reliable information about child labour, poverty, hunger and access to education easier for students and families to understand, and connect each issue to organizations’ own programmes and reporting.

## Investigate

- Reviewed how poverty, child labour and interrupted education can reinforce one another.
- Organized the website around four issues and selected organizations with public programme information.
- **Evidence to attach:** research notes, source list and an early site map.
- **Personal reflection prompt:** What surprised you about the causes of child labour? Which sources helped you judge claims?

## Prepare

- Planned an issue key, project directory, outcome stories, safe-giving guidance and accessible page navigation.
- Checked that outcome numbers are organization-reported, include a time period where available, and link to the source.
- **Evidence to attach:** draft wireframe, content outline and photo/illustration credits.
- **Personal reflection prompt:** What did you change after planning or feedback?

## Act

- Restored the repository page to its original committed version before beginning this revision.
- Redesigned the page with a warm, readable visual system and a responsive left-side issue legend.
- Added reduced-motion-aware scroll reveals and replaced the hero photograph with an original issue infographic.
- Added a credited, openly licensed photograph of well drilling in progress, alongside a separate documented example of a completed well project.
- Added organization-reported outcome examples with links to their source reports and context about how to interpret the figures.
- **Evidence to attach:** dated screenshots, the final page link, and notes on which sections or assets you personally created or adapted.
- **Personal reflection prompt:** Who could use this site? How did you decide what information to include? What did you learn about presenting another organization’s results fairly?

## Reflect

- **Complete after sharing the site:** record feedback from students, teachers or family; note what they found clear or confusing.
- **Your reflection:** What worked? What would you revise after seeing people use it? What new question did the work raise?
- **Measure:** record a real measure if available, such as a class presentation, feedback responses or visits. Do not estimate impact without evidence.

## Demonstrate

- Present the site and explain the relationship between an issue, an organization’s work and a reported outcome.
- Save this record, source links and dated screenshots in your Service as Action portfolio.
- **Evidence to attach:** presentation slides or notes, audience feedback and a final screenshot.
